
<h2>About me</h2><br>

<p>I am <b>Raquel</b>, a linguist with a degree in English Studies, a Master's in Digital Humanities, and several years of experience teaching English in secondary education.</p>

<p>My teaching experience has involved working with language from both theoretical and practical perspectives: designing learning materials, assessing language proficiency, and adapting linguistic content to different levels and learning needs. This has strengthened my understanding of language structure, acquisition, and use, while developing my analytical and communication skills.</p>

<p>My interest in the relationship between language and technology has gradually led me towards <b>computational linguistics, natural language processing (NLP), and corpus-based linguistic analysis</b>.</p>

<p>More recently, I have focused on applying computational methods to linguistic research. For my Master's thesis, I developed an experimental prototype to identify linguistic features associated with misinformation in Spanish-language posts on X. This involved corpus compilation and annotation, linguistic feature extraction, text classification using TF-IDF and logistic regression, and the development of an interactive application with Python and Streamlit.</p>

<p>I have also received training in conversational interface design and chatbot development, working with Dialogflow, dialogue flows, intents, and entities.</p>
